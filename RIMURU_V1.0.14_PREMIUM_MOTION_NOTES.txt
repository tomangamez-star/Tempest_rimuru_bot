RIMURU / JTF CARDS — V1.0.14 PREMIUM MOTION

WHAT LANDED
- Premium Motion is an extension of all four existing /crender styles.
- It is available only after choosing T6.
- Static T6 remains available with the existing rules and coin pricing.
- Premium Animated T6 costs exactly 10 Telegram Stars (XTR).
- Accepted inputs: Telegram GIF/animation, MP4, MOV, or WEBM.
- Input guardrails: maximum 8 seconds and 15 MB.
- Output: silent 700x900, 24 FPS, 6-second H.264 MP4 animation.
- The uploaded animation remains visibly moving beneath the completed card.
- Each renderer adds its own colour identity, moving stars, glow and shimmer.
- Animated cards can be saved and viewed through /customcards and /cview.
- Animated saved cards cannot become static /char or spawn overrides.

PAYMENT SAFETY
- Telegram pre-checkout validates the live user session, payload, XTR currency,
  and exact 10-Star amount before approving payment.
- Telegram charge IDs are unique and idempotent; duplicate payment updates do
  not create duplicate renders.
- Payment state is durable in SQLite and mirrored immediately to Postgres:
  paid -> rendering -> delivered, or refunded/refund_pending on failure.
- If rendering fails after payment, Rimuru calls refundStarPayment automatically.
- If Render restarts after pre-checkout but before delivery, a late payment with
  no live render session is rejected/refunded rather than silently consumed.

DEPLOYMENT
- No new environment variables are required.
- ffmpeg-static is installed through package.json/package-lock.json.
- ALLOWED_UPDATES automatically includes message, callback_query, and
  pre_checkout_query; legacy "messages" is normalized to "message".
