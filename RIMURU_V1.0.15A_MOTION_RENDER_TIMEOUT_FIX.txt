RIMURU v1.0.15A — MOTION RENDER TIMEOUT FIX

Production evidence:
[crender] render failed: animation rendering timed out

ROOT CAUSE
The live request was decoding 1280x720 30fps border sources (including 10-bit
4:4:4 sources), cropping, rotating, scaling, black-keying, drawing six
particles, compositing five layers, and encoding the result. Render's CPU
could not complete that pipeline inside the 120-second safety timeout.

FIX
- All four border loops are now pre-baked to 700x900 portrait.
- Assets are six seconds, 20fps, H.264, and yuv420p.
- Border particles are baked into the assets once instead of drawn per render.
- Live requests no longer rotate/crop the border or convert 10-bit video.
- The inexpensive black-key remains live so artwork colors are not washed out.
- Output uses the ultrafast H.264 preset at CRF 22.
- Artwork motion, random border selection, VIP crest, owner signature,
  foil sweep, payments, refunds, and owner-free behavior are unchanged.
- No new environment variables.

VALIDATION
- Full real six-second compositor test passed.
- Output: 700x900, 20fps, yuv420p H.264 MP4.
- Local end-to-end benchmark: 8.8 seconds including test-video generation,
  static Old Gen rendering, badge generation, and final encode.
- Syntax and focused Premium Motion tests passed.
